## Course Overview & Key Domains

Google's **AI for Research and Insights** provides rigorous methodologies for synthesizing complex multi-modal data streams, executing autonomous multi-step web research with **Gemini Deep Research**, building centralized research repositories with **Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)**, designing custom expert personas, and strictly verifying findings against ground-truth source citations.

### Core Modules:
1. **Module 1: Build a Research Report with Gemini Deep Research**
   - Deep Research Workflows: Formulating multi-faceted research queries that trigger iterative external exploration.
   - Information Synthesis: Transitioning raw research reports into actionable operational deliverables (e.g., event schedules, market vendor deadlines).
   - Real-World Fact-Checking: Auditing external dates, regulatory filings, and market requirements.

2. **Module 2: Create and Share Research Summaries in Gemini Notebook**
   - Multimodal Source Consolidation: Integrating heterogeneous sources (PDF research papers, YouTube video lectures, web documentation) inside a single Notebook.
   - Grounded Cross-Document Querying: Asking the model to synthesize high-level 1-page executive briefs across all loaded modalities simultaneously.
   - Citation Verification: Tracing claims directly back to their source document page numbers and text snippets via clickable citation anchors.

3. **Module 3: Build an Expert Advisor with Gemini**
   - Meta-Prompting & Persona Design: Structuring expert personas with specific domain expertise, behavioral characteristics, and evaluation methodologies.
   - Injecting Specific Context: Grounding the persona with project goals, target audience, and operational constraints to prevent bland or generic output.
   - Pressure-Testing Agendas & Constrained Plans: Simulating expert evaluators to stress-test complex workflows under strict operational constraints.
