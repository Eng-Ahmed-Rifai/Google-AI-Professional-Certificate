## Course Overview & Key Domains

Google's **AI for Data Analysis** equips practitioners to leverage Gemini directly within spreadsheet applications (Google Sheets) to clean unstructured data, run conversational AI formulas (`=AI()`), extract tabular schemas from screenshots/images, and produce intuitive visual charts.

### Core Modules:
1. **Module 1: Extract & Structure Data with Multimodal AI**
   - Multimodal OCR to Tabular Schema: Extracting unstructured review screenshots directly into multi-column structured tables (e.g. date, review text, star rating) ready for Google Sheets analysis.
   - Goal-Driven Metric Identification: Aligning multi-platform marketing datasets with top-level executive business goals (e.g., brand awareness vs direct conversions).

2. **Module 2: Natural Language Formulas with `=AI()` in Sheets**
   - Natural Language Spreadsheet Functions: Constructing `=AI(prompt, cell_reference)` formulas to classify freeform customer feedback into categorical buckets (`fit`, `quality`, or `style`) for quantitative analysis.
   - Aggregate Engagement Summaries: Prompting Gemini in Sheets for rapid macro summaries (total completions, total hours spent).

3. **Module 3: Data Verification, Auditing & Visualization**
   - Ground-Truth Sampling: Verifying AI-generated attribution summaries against raw transactional referral data to ensure accuracy before budget reallocations.
   - Rapid Visual Analytics: Generating bar charts and visual comparisons of average revenue per category to share findings with operational teams.
