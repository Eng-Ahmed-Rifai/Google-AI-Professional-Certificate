## Course Overview & Key Domains

Google's **AI for Content Creation** explores generative media workflows (Imagen, Veo, Gemini in Google Slides), multimodal prompt crafting for image/video generation, collaborative design ideation, and structured visual critique frameworks.

### Core Modules:
1. **Module 1: Generate Visual Assets with Imagen and Veo**
   - Image Generation with Imagen: Crafting granular text-to-image prompts specifying subject, aesthetic style (e.g. modern 3D illustration), color palettes, and lighting.
   - Video Generation with Veo: Utilizing image-to-video capabilities by attaching reference characters/mascots and prompting precise motion and contextual narrative.

2. **Module 2: Presentation Design & Collaborative Ideation**
   - Gemini in Google Slides: Deconstructing text-heavy, cognitive-load slides into concise visual hierarchies.
   - Collaborative Ideation: Prompting AI to offer distinct sets of headlines and descriptions rather than static rewrites, enabling human curation.

3. **Module 3: Objective Design Critique & Brand Alignment**
   - Goal-Grounded Critique: Evaluating draft creative assets against explicit project goals and target themes (e.g., casual picnic vs corporate) to generate actionable feedback for designers.
   - Infographic & Timeline Auditing: Evaluating visual infographics for clarity, stakeholder comprehension, and actionable weak-spot identification.
