## Course Overview & Key Domains

Google's **AI for Brainstorming and Planning** develops advanced competencies in leveraging Generative AI (Google Gemini, NotebookLM) as an interactive strategic collaborator for creative ideation, operational planning, schedule auditing, risk discovery, and centralized project knowledge management.

### Core Modules:
1. **Module 1: Brainstorming with AI**
   - Collaborative Ideation: Shifting from static prompt generation to multi-turn dialogue with AI.
   - Persona & Role Simulation: Instructing Gemini to adopt specialized personas (e.g., Devil's Advocate, Customer Archetypes, Domain Experts) to uncover blind spots and challenge assumptions.
   - Evaluation Frameworks: Structuring brainstormed outputs into multi-attribute decision matrices (e.g., comparing ideas by search volume, competition, and strategic alignment).

2. **Module 2: Project Planning and Scoping with AI**
   - Scope Breakdown: Decomposing large, ambiguous project goals into concrete work packages and milestones.
   - Schedule & Risk Auditing: Using AI to analyze complex schedules for hidden operational risks, overlap constraints, and resource bottlenecks (such as double-booked venues or simultaneous assignments).
   - Dynamic Timeline Re-baselining: Prompting AI to conduct impact analysis when project dependencies shift and automatically recalculate milestone schedules.

3. **Module 3: Knowledge Management with NotebookLM**
   - Grounded Knowledge Hubs: Creating centralized, authoritative project notebooks in NotebookLM.
   - Multi-Document Synthesis: Grounding AI queries strictly within uploaded company documentation (HR policies, technical specs, project timelines) to eliminate hallucinations.
   - Enabling Self-Serve Collaboration: Sharing interactive NotebookLM environments with onboarding team members for independent, cited query resolution.
