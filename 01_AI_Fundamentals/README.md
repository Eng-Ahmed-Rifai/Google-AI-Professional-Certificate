## Course Overview & Key Domains

Google's **AI Fundamentals** establishes core practical competency in applying generative artificial intelligence tools (such as Google Gemini) to workplace productivity, strategic workflows, and ethical problem solving.

### Core Modules:
1. **Module 1: Welcome to the Course & Generative AI Fundamentals**
   - Defining Generative AI vs Traditional Machine Learning.
   - Large Language Models (LLMs) and foundation architectures.
   - Training paradigms: pre-training on massive multi-modal corpora, pattern recognition, and token prediction.

2. **Module 2: Prompt Engineering Architecture**
   - The 4-Pillar Prompt Framework:
     - **Task:** Explicit action verb and clear end goal.
     - **Context:** Background, persona, target audience, and business situation.
     - **References:** Source data, style guides, baseline examples, or input documents.
     - **Constraints:** Format requirements, tone, character/word limits, and things to avoid.
   - Iterative prompting strategies: Refining, editing prior prompts in conversational threads, and multimodal prompting (combining text + imagery).

3. **Module 3: Evaluating AI Outputs & Responsible AI**
   - Spotting hallucinations, bias, and inaccuracies.
   - The **"Check" Phase**: Cross-referencing AI claims against raw ground-truth source documentation.
   - Privacy & Security Best Practices: Redacting sensitive data, anonymizing personal/corporate identifiers (`[Internal IP]`, `[User ID]`).
   - Grounding mechanisms: Utilizing AI research links and real-time verifiable search citations.

4. **Module 4: Practical Workflow Integration**
   - Customer support response drafts, policy summaries, meeting synthesis.
   - Aligning tone and brand voice across marketing copy.
   - Building human-in-the-loop workflows where AI proposes and humans curate.
