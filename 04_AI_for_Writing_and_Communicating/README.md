## Course Overview & Key Domains

Google's **AI for Writing and Communicating** masters workplace communication using AI across Google Workspace (Gemini in Google Meet, Gemini Canvas, and Gemini Live).

### Core Modules:
1. **Module 1: Keep Your Teams on Track with Gemini**
   - Automated Note-Taking in Google Meet: Enabling the native note-taking and transcription features in Meet to eliminate distractions from manual notes.
   - Post-Meeting Action Extraction: Transforming dense meeting transcripts into structured action item matrices (Action item, Owner, Deadline).

2. **Module 2: Pressure-Test Ideas in Gemini**
   - Simulating Stakeholder Objections: Directing Gemini to adopt specific investor or executive personas (e.g., investor focusing on financial returns and operational feasibility) to surface hidden risks in pitch decks.
   - Rehearsal & Tone Analysis with Gemini Live: Defining difficult conversation scenarios (e.g. project delays) and verbally practicing with Gemini Live to get real-time feedback on delivery tone and problem-solving.

3. **Module 3: Draft Communications with Gemini Canvas**
   - Canvas Document Composition: Utilizing Gemini Canvas to draft, edit, and tailor messages to specific target audiences.
   - Persona & Priority Constraints: Carrying forward audience-specific priorities (e.g. aesthetic trends for bloggers vs unit pricing for wholesale suppliers) into Canvas prompts.

4. **Module 4: Get Speech Feedback from Gemini Live**
   - Verbal Communication Practice: Rehearsing elevator pitches and formal client updates.
   - Actionable Feedback Loops: Extracting targeted written critique on clarity, pacing, and executive presence.
